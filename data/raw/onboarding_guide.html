New Hire Onboarding Guide

Welcome to the company. This guide covers what to expect during your first two weeks.

Day 1: Setup

Your manager will meet you at 9am to walk through IT setup. You will receive a laptop, badge, and access to Slack, email, and the internal wiki.

  • Complete I-9 and tax forms in the HR portal
  • Set up multi-factor authentication on your company email
  • Join the #new-hires Slack channel

Week 1: Training

You will complete mandatory compliance training covering data privacy, workplace conduct, and security awareness. Training must be finished by end of week 1.

Week 2: Team Integration

You will be paired with an onboarding buddy from your team for your first two weeks. Your buddy will help you navigate team processes and answer day-to-day questions.

Benefits Enrollment

Benefits enrollment must be completed within 30 days of your start date. This includes health insurance, dental, vision, and 401k elections. Contact HR at hr@company.com with questions.
